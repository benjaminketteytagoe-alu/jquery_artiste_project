Frequently Asked Questions

How can I submit an artiste to the gallery?

Use the contact form above and select "Artiste Submission" as the subject. Provide the artiste's name, genre, photo, and a brief bio. Our curation team reviews all submissions within 48 hours.

Is Artiste Gallery free to use?

Yes! Browsing and searching our artiste collection is completely free. We offer premium features for artistes who want enhanced profiles and analytics.

How do I report an issue or suggest a feature?

Reach out via our contact form with "Technical Support" or "Feedback & Suggestions" as the subject. We value community input and review every message personally.

Can I partner with Artiste Gallery?

Absolutely! We welcome partnership inquiries from venues, record labels, media outlets, and event organizers. Select "Partnership Opportunity" in the contact form to start the conversation.
